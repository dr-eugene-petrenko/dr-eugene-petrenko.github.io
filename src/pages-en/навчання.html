For doctors for doctors

I teach at the Department of Obstetrics, Gynecology and Perinatology, Faculty of Postgraduate Education, Dnipro State Medical University.

"Caesarean Section" training — basic course

4 days, 3–4 topics a day. Perioperative preparation, surgical anatomy, step-by-step operative technique, simulator practice.

"Caesarean Section" training — advanced course

4 days. Complex clinical situations, types of caesarean section, case reviews, assessment.

Teaching guidelines

Part 1 — perioperative preparation following ERAS and leading guidelines; Part 2 — types of caesarean section, surgical anatomy, technique.

Who it is for: residents (interns), obstetrician-gynecologists, anaesthetists, neonatologists, midwives and theatre nurses.
