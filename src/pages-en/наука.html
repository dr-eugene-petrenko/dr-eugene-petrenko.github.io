My position

Caesarean section without medical indication, at the woman's request, should be limited. Modern caesarean section is performed safely, with modern approaches and materials. But "everything is a medicine, everything is a poison": the art of modern obstetrics lies in constantly weighing risks rather than creating additional ones.
