What the maternity hospital offers

🤰 Antenatal care

From the first weeks until birth. Outpatient antenatal care is free of charge under Ukraine's National Health Service (NHSU) Medical Guarantees Program.

👨‍👩‍👧 Partner-supported birth

Private birth rooms: your husband or another close person can be with you.

🩺 Labour ward

12 birth rooms, freedom of movement and position in labour, rooming-in for mother and baby. Obstetrician-gynecologists are on duty around the clock. The center specialises in births complicated by severe obstetric and medical conditions.

🛏️ Antenatal (high-risk pregnancy) ward

A department for women who need monitoring and treatment for pregnancy complications. Management of rhesus-sensitised pregnancies.

🚑 Emergency care

The emergency obstetric and gynecological care department works 24/7.

👶 Newborn care

Neonatal intensive care with a neonatal transport team, a newborn unit with an intensive care area, care of preterm babies.

Why it matters

When the labour ward, neonatal intensive care and other specialists are under one roof, mother and baby get care without losing time. That is what safety in childbirth looks like.

  • Modern equipment for resuscitation and care of preterm babies
  • A neonatal transport team that brings babies in from across the region
  • The team: obstetrician-gynecologists, neonatologists, anaesthetists, rehabilitation specialists

Neonatal intensive care

Care for the smallest patients — preterm babies and newborns in critical condition. The center has a full neonatal service made up of several departments that hand the baby on to one another as they recover.

🚑 Intensive care with a transport team

  • In operation since 2009, level 3+ care
  • The neonatal transport team brings babies in from the region's maternity hospitals around the clock
  • 18 neonatal and 3 surgical beds
  • Operations are performed right in the unit, without moving the baby
  • CT, MRI, endoscopy and an operating theatre are available 24/7
  • Complex cases are discussed online with doctors from abroad

🫁 Newborn resuscitation

  • Round-the-clock monitoring of the babies
  • Mechanical ventilation, invasive and non-invasive
  • A transport incubator with a ventilator for safe transfer
  • The team holds international resuscitation certificates

🤱 Care after intensive care

  • Mum stays with her baby the whole time
  • Kangaroo care and skin-to-skin contact
  • More than 400 babies a year, including babies weighing from 500 g
  • After discharge — follow-up at the developmental follow-up clinic

For parents: visiting the neonatal intensive care unit

  • Parents or legal guardians may visit with the on-call doctor's permission and an ID document
  • Before entering, take off your outerwear and use hand sanitiser
  • Do not visit if you have cold symptoms or a fever
  • You may touch your baby and take part in care with the staff's permission
  • Information about the baby's condition is given by the attending doctor or the head of the unit
